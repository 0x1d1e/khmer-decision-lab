## Next research

No screened source provides an independent Khmer decision benchmark with verified rights, suitable labels, and a defensible untouched split. R014 did not change this: SEA-NLI is a gated but public-label, machine-generated NLI set with no train or validation partition, so it served as an unseen-task probe rather than a benchmark, and R014's own result was inconclusive at 37 clusters. R014 also showed that the current lightweight decision models are close to label-prior behaviour on Khmer NLI, so a rights-cleared naturalistic Khmer intent source remains the binding need; an inference-only probe on another small public set would not add much. Candidates screened so far fail different gates:

- [Khmer Emotional Text](https://huggingface.co/datasets/phonsobon/Text_Emotional_kh) declares Apache-2.0 but requires access acceptance; its independent split is unverified, and emotion labels do not measure service intent.
- [Pisethan Khmer Instruct](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Pisethan/khmer-instruct-dataset) declares MIT and has 500 prompt/response pairs in one train split. It has no intent labels or held-out partition.
- [SeyhaLite Khmer Q-A-M](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SeyhaLite/Q-A-M-All-Khmer-Conversations-200k) declares Apache-2.0, but has Q&A rather than intent labels, no documented evaluation split, and unclear source provenance. Its card and listing also disagree on size.
- [nphearum Khmer Raw Text](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nphearum/khmer-raw-text-3M) declares Apache-2.0 and has topic/task-type metadata, but the card says it has no human supervised labels; it has one train split and limited source detail.
- [CADT-IDRI Khmer News](https://huggingface.co/datasets/CADT-IDRI/Khmer_News_classification) lacks a stated license and its source family was used in R004. [GKLMIP news](https://huggingface.co/datasets/SEACrowd/gklmip_newsclass) lists its license as unknown and overlaps prior project work. The CC BY-NC [Khmer ASAG benchmark](https://huggingface.co/datasets/phorknorak/khmer-asag-benchmark) was used in R007. The MIT-licensed [KHOB OCR benchmark](https://github.com/EKYCSolutions/khmer-ocr-benchmark-dataset) has transcription labels, not decision labels. [Bitext Customer Support](https://github.com/bitext/customer-support-llm-chatbot-training-dataset) has 27 English intent labels under CDLA-Sharing-1.0, but is not a Khmer benchmark; adaptation would need fluent native review and a defensible Khmer split. The CC BY-SA 4.0 [DDD Khmer speech dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Digital-Divide-Data/khmer-speech-dataset) has speech/transcription rather than intent labels. [ActableAI Khmer ASR](https://huggingface.co/datasets/actableai/data-khmer) lists its license as “other,” with recording provenance and consent unresolved. A [commercial Khmer call-center dataset](https://datatoorder.com/datasets/khmer-call-center-speech/) is a possible acquisition lead, but its terms, provenance, naturalistic status, and annotation quality are unverified. The [Khmer Polarity paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09313) has not established a usable rights-cleared dataset or independent split in this screen.

A later confirmatory path is frozen evaluation on naturally occurring Khmer service-intent requests from a rights-cleared first-party source, comparing both fixed R006 seeds with the matching multilingual base. This needs a source and fluent Khmer annotators; neither is secured.

Before collection, secure written research, privacy, retention, and publication rights; record source and collection period; remove direct identifiers; and exclude sensitive records unless separately approved. Recruit fluent native Khmer annotators. Define one request per item and a taxonomy for primary, multiple, ambiguous, and out-of-scope intent. Pilot and freeze the guide, double-annotate without model outputs, adjudicate disagreements, report agreement, and set an agreement gate and independent-group target. Keep the pilot out of the locked test.

Group by user, case/thread, intake channel or source batch, template family, and normalized near-duplicates. Assign whole groups to chronological train/validation/test periods. This proposed confirmatory comparison is frozen evaluation: no target training, calibration, or checkpoint selection. Keep the test labels and scores sealed until source terms, taxonomy, group/time split, model identities, and analysis are locked. Do not use model predictions as gold labels or describe generated/translated examples as naturalistic user data. If user or source IDs are unavailable, do not claim those forms of independence.

No partner source or native annotators are secured. Reassess source terms and label quality before pursuing the confirmatory collection path. R013's completed MASSIVE-based study and R014's SEA-NLI probe are exploratory evidence and do not satisfy the independent-benchmark requirement.
